Overview

The Skeptic is a local party game. It runs entirely on your device and does not connect to any server, collect any personal information, or transmit any data anywhere. We collect nothing.

Data We Collect

None. Player names you enter exist only in memory for the duration of the session and are never saved to disk or sent anywhere. The app works completely offline.

Permissions

Vibration — haptic feedback during gameplay. No data collected.

Wake Lock — keeps screen on during the discussion timer. No data collected.

In-App Purchases — handled entirely by Google Play / Apple App Store. We never see or store payment information.

Adults Category

The Adults category contains content suitable only for players aged 18 and above. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising use of this category. The Family version of The Skeptic does not include adult content.
