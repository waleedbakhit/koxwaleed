Privacy Policy — Last updated: May 2026

Overview

The Skeptic is a local party game. It runs entirely on your device and does not connect to any server, collect any personal information, or transmit any data anywhere.

Data We Collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Player names you enter during the game exist only in memory for the duration of the session and are never saved to disk or sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app requests two permissions:

Vibration — used to provide haptic feedback during gameplay events such as voting and role reveals. No data is collected through this permission.

Wake Lock — used to keep the screen on during the discussion timer so the game is not interrupted. No data is collected through this permission.

Third-Party Services

The Skeptic does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no ads in this app.

Children

The Skeptic is intended for general audiences. However, the Adults category within the game contains content suitable only for players aged 18 and above. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising use of the Adults category.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL. Changes take effect immediately upon posting.
